# Ethics Checklist for Developers

Step 1 of the [Digital Heritage Ethics Workflow](README.md). This is not an exhaustive list but a
set of questions to revisit throughout design and implementation — at minimum during system
design and again during user testing, to catch unintentional harms early. Document your answers
as paradata for future accountability.

## Participation and Inclusivity

1. Can users/community see or add alternative names, interpretations, or perspectives?
2. Is the interface usable and accessible for the intended end users?

## Transparency

3. Can the source, provenance, and transformation history be traced?
4. Is AI/model/tool use documented and visible?

## Accountability

5. Are sensitive, harmful, or contested cases flagged and handled with warnings or safeguards?
6. Can a human intervene with AI/automated workflows and review or respond to complaints?

## Trustworthiness

7. Is the difference between original record, inference, and reconstruction clearly shown?
8. Is uncertainty clearly labelled?

## Sustainability

9. Are licence, consent, and reuse conditions clear?
10. Is enough documentation preserved for future reuse?

## Feasibility and Balance

11. Are intended workflow elements necessary and proportionate to ethical risks?
12. Are compliance requirements and ethical trade-offs documented?

---

**If all checks are met:** proceed to Step 4, [Digital Heritage Ethics Declaration](digital-heritage-ethics-declaration.md).

**If some checks are not met:** proceed to Step 2, the [Ethics Risks and Best Practice Tool](https://infinity-ethics.designinformatics.org/).
