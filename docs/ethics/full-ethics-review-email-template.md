# Full Ethics Review — email template

Step 3 of the [Digital Heritage Ethics Workflow](README.md). Use this template to escalate
unresolved ethical risks to the Executive Board after Steps 1 and 2 have not fully resolved them.
Record the outcome in Step 3 of the [Digital Heritage Ethics Declaration](digital-heritage-ethics-declaration.md).

---

Subject: Full Ethics Review request — semRTI

Dear [insert name/s of Executive Board],

risks associated with semRTI.

**Description of the risks and potential harms:**

[insert description]

**Proposed mitigation:**

[insert proposed mitigation]

Please could you indicate whether we have your approval to proceed on this basis, or advise on
further steps required.

From [name of Champion]

---

Record the outcome (approval granted/denied, and the name of the authority who granted it) in
Step 3 of the [Digital Heritage Ethics Declaration](digital-heritage-ethics-declaration.md).
