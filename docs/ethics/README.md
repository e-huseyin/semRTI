# Digital Heritage Ethics Workflow

All project outputs (mKG, ontology, annotated corpus, datasets, AI training data, model,
pipeline, API, plug-in, software, scientific report) must be accompanied by a completed Minimum
Ethical, FAIR, and CARE Compliance Checklist (see the INFINITY Data Management Plan). Software
submitted to the Technical Board must additionally complete this Digital Heritage Ethics Workflow
and be accompanied by a **Digital Heritage Ethics Declaration**.

Consult this workflow at the beginning of the development process and throughout, as a
self-reflection tool for ethical risks and decisions. It applies to every tool, service, and
output in INFINITY. Ethical considerations should be discussed by the whole team, even though
developers lead the technical implementation.

## Steps

You must complete **Step 1 OR Step 2 OR Step 3**, and always **Steps 4 and 5**.

1. **[Ethics Checklist for developers](ethics-checklist.md)** — read throughout development;
   assess whether all 12 checks (across 6 principles) are met.
   - All met → go to Step 4.
   - Not all met → go to Step 2.
2. **Ethics Risks and Best Practice Tool** — https://infinity-ethics.designinformatics.org/.
   Covers the same six principles (Participation and Inclusivity, Transparency, Accountability,
   Trustworthiness, Sustainability, Feasibility and Balance) with associated risks and best
   practices. If a risk is medium/high for your project, check the associated best practices and
   use the resulting checklist as a discussion starting point.
   - All met → go to Step 4.
   - Not met → go to Step 3.
3. **Full Ethics Review** — required if unacceptable ethical risks persist or cannot be
   mitigated. Escalate to the Executive Board using the
   [email template](full-ethics-review-email-template.md) and obtain sign-off. Then go to Step 4.
4. **[Digital Heritage Ethics Declaration](digital-heritage-ethics-declaration.md)** — complete
   and submit to the Technical Board along with the INFINITY component.
5. **Revisit before every release** — before submitting a component (i.e. any new version) to the
   Technical Board, revisit the Declaration, ensure it is current, and resubmit.
